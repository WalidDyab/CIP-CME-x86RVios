Alignment Philosophy

The national NQF, PSU Graduate Attributes / Institutional Learning Outcomes, ABET Student Outcomes, CLOs, and assessment evidence are different layers. This page treats crosswalks as curriculum-alignment tools, not as official NQF–ABET equivalencies.

Reference Documents & Methodology

The 2026 NQF document defines the new national Level 6 framework. PSU documents describe the university's current/previous NQF implementation and mapping philosophy. PSU Graduate Attributes / ILOs provide the institutional layer, while ABET SOs provide the engineering accreditation layer.

The crosswalk tables are curriculum-alignment tools. They should not be represented as official NQF-ABET mappings unless such mappings are explicitly documented by the relevant authority.
