NQF 2026 Transition & Curriculum Alignment

From national qualification requirements to institutional outcomes, ABET Student Outcomes, course CLOs, and assessment evidence.

The NQF defines national qualification expectations; PSU Graduate Attributes and ILOs provide the institutional interpretation; engineering PLOs and ABET SOs form the program layer; CLOs implement those requirements within courses; and assessment supplies evidence of attainment. These are distinct alignment layers—not equivalent or interchangeable frameworks.

Section D — New NQF → ILO/GA → Engineering PLO / ABET SO

The ABET Student Outcomes SO1–SO7 remain unchanged. The 2026 NQF revision does not require creation or modification of the ABET SO structure; the task is to map the new Level 6 descriptors and subskills to the existing SO1–SO7 where meaningful. No SO8 is introduced for AI, cybersecurity, or digital skills. A requirement may span several existing SOs or have no exact one-to-one ABET equivalent.

Reference Documents & Methodology

The 2026 NQF document defines the new national Level 6 framework. PSU documents describe the university's current/previous NQF implementation and mapping philosophy. PSU Graduate Attributes / ILOs provide the institutional layer, while ABET SOs provide the engineering accreditation layer.

Crosswalk tables are curriculum-alignment tools, not official NQF–ABET mappings unless explicitly documented by the relevant authority.
