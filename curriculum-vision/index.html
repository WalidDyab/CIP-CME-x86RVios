Current Curriculum · Continuous Improvement

CLO Review and ABET Assessment Alignment

The Bachelor of Science in Electrical Engineering program is accredited by the Engineering Accreditation Commission of ABET, www.abet.org.

The CLO review began in December 2025 as part of the program's continuous-improvement response to ABET assessment discussions. Using the Term 251 submission as the baseline, faculty review and refinement continued through Term 252 and subsequent stages, leading to the proposed Term 261 CLO framework.

Current Curriculum · Assessment Evidence

CLO–SO–PI Alignment & Evidence Analysis

See how each ABET Student Outcome is served by the required curriculum, which CLOs and Performance Indicators carry it, and where the approved mappings would benefit from stronger assessment evidence. Program-level conclusions are drawn from core courses only, since they represent the curriculum every student takes; elective mappings stay available for exploration. The analysis is supportive by design: it starts from the assumption that every approved mapping is intentional, and recommends remapping only where there is a clear conceptual mismatch.
