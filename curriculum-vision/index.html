Curriculum Committee Vision & Future Direction

Building a modern, data-driven, and digitally integrated curriculum ecosystem.

Our Mission

The Curriculum Committee aims to transform curriculum management from static documents and disconnected workflows into an integrated digital ecosystem that supports continuous improvement, assessment automation, and curriculum intelligence.

Strategic Direction

Historical Course Data Integration

Link historical course folders, reports, assessment records, and course revisions into the curriculum portal.

Curriculum Analytics

Develop dashboards and analytical tools for CLO coverage, SO mapping, curriculum gaps, and longitudinal assessment analysis.

Assessment Automation

Design assessment methods that support computerized evaluation and automated analytics.

Digital Examination Ecosystem

Expand the use of digital exams and integrated assessment systems such as ExamSoft.

AI-Assisted Curriculum Intelligence

Leverage AI tools to support curriculum review, assessment design, analytics, and continuous improvement processes.

Why This Matters

Engineering education can no longer ignore the digital opportunities available today. The committee believes that curriculum systems should become dynamic, analyzable, connected, and continuously improvable.

Long-Term Vision

  • Unified curriculum intelligence platform
  • Automated accreditation support
  • Integrated assessment analytics
  • Faculty collaboration ecosystem
  • Data-driven curriculum improvement
  • Digital-first assessment strategies
