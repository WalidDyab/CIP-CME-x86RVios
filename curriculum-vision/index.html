Current Curriculum · Continuous Improvement

Continuous Improvement of the EE Curriculum

The Undergraduate EE curriculum is periodically reviewed using assessment evidence, accreditation feedback, curriculum review, and stakeholder and faculty input. Following ABET feedback concerning Student Outcome assessment, the program strengthened the CLO framework through explicit alignment with ABET Student Outcomes and Performance Indicators. Selected CLOs were modified, added, or omitted as part of the Term 252 targeted improvement.

Institutional Curriculum Direction

Major Curriculum Revision

The major curriculum revision is a separate initiative undertaken in response to a wider institutional policy and may include substantial changes to the Undergraduate EE curriculum. Its scope and supporting documentation will be presented independently as the institutional revision develops.
