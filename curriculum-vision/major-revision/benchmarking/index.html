Scope and method

How to read the comparison

The Saudi comparison uses the four-year plan and excludes preparatory-year courses. The international comparison treats the university-level block as general education, breadth, writing, or liberal-studies requirements and distinguishes named prescribed courses from requirements chosen by students. PSU is the reference institution, not one of the 13 comparators.

Executive reading

Key findings

PSU prescribes the full university block

All 23 PSU university-level credits are named courses. Every international comparator shown gives students 100% choice within its equivalent breadth block.

Arabic and Islamic studies are comparatively high

PSU assigns 14 credits to the combined area, matching KAU and exceeding KFUPM, PMU, and KSU at 8; Alfaisal shows none.

Degree length is above most comparators

PSU’s 138-credit degree is higher than all international programs shown (120–129) and most Saudi programs, while PMU reaches 139.

Flexibility is concentrated elsewhere

PSU shows 6 technical-elective credits and no student-chosen university breadth. Saudi comparators range from 6 to 30 technical-elective credits.

PSU curriculum anatomy

The source deck includes detailed PSU plans for the College of Engineering and College of Computer & Information Sciences. For engineering, each listed program uses the same 138-credit architecture.

University requirements · 23 credits

  • English: ENG101 Intensive Writing (3), PSY101 Introduction to Psychology (3), COM201 Communication Skills (3)
  • Arabic: ARAB101 Arabic Writing (2), ARAB103 Professional and Literary Writing (2), ARAB203 Arabic Grammar (2)
  • Islamic studies: ISC101 Islamic Ethics (2), ISC103 Islamic Economic System (2), ISC105 Studies of the Holy Quran (2), ISC203 New Financial Transactions (2)

Engineering college core · 42 credits

  • Programming, chemistry, statistics, calculus I–II, differential equations, physics I–II
  • Technical writing and translation, engineering drawing and design
  • Ethics and social aspects of engineering; engineering economy

Electrical Engineering · 63 credits

  • Engineering mathematics, linear algebra, project management
  • Circuits, electronics, logic design, signals, machines, power electronics and power systems
  • Microprocessors, control, electromagnetics, communications, senior design project
  • Two 3-credit program electives

CCIS reference · 134 credits

  • 23 university + 34 college + 67 program + 10 internship credits
  • CS program detail: 41 core, 15 CS electives, 3 business background, 6 free electives, 2 physical education, and 10 internship credits
  • AI/Data Science, Machine Learning, Digital Image Processing, and NLP appear among selectable CS electives
For committee consideration

Implications for the major revision

  • Review whether some university requirements can move from named courses to controlled choice while preserving PSU’s institutional and national obligations.
  • Evaluate the 138-credit total against the 120–129-credit international range and the 128–139-credit Saudi range shown in the deck.
  • Consider whether technical-elective capacity is sufficient for emerging areas and student specialization.
  • Preserve the value of PSU’s substantial cooperative education while testing its credit weight and duration against comparator models.
  • Use stakeholder feedback and impact analysis before converting benchmarking observations into approved curriculum changes.

These are evidence-led discussion points, not approved committee decisions. The source presentation remains the authoritative artifact for this phase.
