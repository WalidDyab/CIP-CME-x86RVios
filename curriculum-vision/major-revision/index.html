Major Curriculum Revision Progress

Prince Sultan University’s Electrical Engineering program is undertaking a major curriculum revision to ensure continued alignment with academic standards, stakeholder expectations, technological developments, and the future needs of the profession. This page provides a concise view of the revision phases, completed work, current activity, and supporting evidence.
