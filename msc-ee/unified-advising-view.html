Flexible program design

One program, four focus areas, and room for cross-disciplinary specialization.

This portal presents the MSc in Electrical Engineering as a flexible ecosystem. Students may build depth in one concentration area, or shape a research theme that combines courses from different areas such as AI, robotics, embedded systems, communications, and sustainable energy.

Advising message

The program is not restricted to a single rigid concentration. A student can pursue a pure track pathway or define a specific topic that strategically draws from multiple focus areas.

Focus Areas

Each concentration area has a distinct identity, but students are also free to combine courses across areas when a research topic requires it.

Student Pathway Examples
