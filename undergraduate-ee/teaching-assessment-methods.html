Professional Ethics Framework

Professional ethics references used to support the teaching and assessment of ethical and professional responsibility, particularly ABET Student Outcome 4 (SO4).

International reference

IEEE Code of Ethics

Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE)

Classification

International / discipline-specific professional ethics reference for Electrical and Electronics Engineering

Citation

Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE), “IEEE Code of Ethics,” IEEE, revised and adopted by the IEEE Board of Directors, June 2020.

Summary

The IEEE Code of Ethics provides the principal professional ethics framework for electrical and electronics engineering. It emphasizes integrity, responsible professional conduct, public safety, health and welfare, sustainable development, privacy, societal implications of technology, conflicts of interest, honesty in technical work, professional competence, fairness, and accountability. Within the curriculum, it serves as a discipline-specific reference for developing students’ ability to recognize ethical and professional responsibilities and make informed engineering judgments.

National reference

Engineer’s Charter (ميثاق المهندس)

Saudi Council of Engineers (SCE) / الهيئة السعودية للمهندسين

Classification

Saudi national professional engineering ethics reference

Citation

Saudi Council of Engineers (SCE), “Engineer’s Charter (ميثاق المهندس),” Saudi Council of Engineers, Saudi Arabia, 2025.

Summary

The Engineer’s Charter establishes the national professional and ethical framework for engineering practice in Saudi Arabia. It emphasizes that engineering practice should be governed by professional rules, ethics, and standards and promotes values including honesty, trustworthiness, integrity, fairness, fulfillment of professional obligations, and excellence in engineering work. It provides an important national reference for professional responsibility and complements the IEEE Code of Ethics within the Electrical Engineering curriculum.

Official document language: Arabic

View Document PDF Official SCE Source ↗

Curriculum use: The IEEE Code of Ethics and the Saudi Council of Engineers’ Engineer’s Charter provide complementary international discipline-specific and national professional references for teaching and assessing ethical and professional responsibility. They support SO4 learning activities involving recognition of ethical responsibilities, analysis of engineering situations, consideration of stakeholder and societal impacts, and informed professional judgment.
