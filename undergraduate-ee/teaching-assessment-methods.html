Course-Specific Teaching Strategies

EE 490 – Senior Design Project

EE 490 uses a course-specific teaching model appropriate to a senior design project and is an approved exception to the general pool.

  • Flipped Classroom
  • Project Studio
  • Laboratory Demonstrations
  • Oral Discussions
  • Team Meetings

EE 492 – Co-Op

EE 492 is delivered primarily through workplace-based experiential learning and therefore uses a course-specific Teaching Strategy.

  • Field Experience

Course-Specific Assessment Methods

EE 490 – Senior Design Project

These methods reflect the staged nature of the Senior Design Project and replace the normal minimum-four-method rule for this course.

  • Written Reports (Proposal, Interim, and Final)
  • Oral Presentations
  • Weekly Progress Assessment

EE 492 – Co-Op

These methods reflect workplace performance and final Co-Op reporting requirements and form an approved exception to the general assessment pool.

  • Employer Assessment
  • Final Report
  • Final Oral Presentation
